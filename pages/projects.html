Bordr

Live

Prepares photos for Instagram: a uniform border, the right format, and a carousel slicer for wide shots.

Bordr adds a uniform white border to your photos and exports them at your preferred ratio. Border width is set from the interface.

Wide photos can be sliced across 2 to 5 carousel slides, so a panorama becomes one continuous swipe. Results are exported together as a zip.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the Canvas API, with JSZip for packaging. Images are processed in your browser and never uploaded, and the page can be hosted statically with no login.

JavaScript · Canvas API · JSZip

Red Odyssey

Completed

Martian terrain exploration and pathfinding web app developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, calculating slope-aware routes across Jezero Crater.

Built around a 20 m/pixel Digital Elevation Model (DEM) and 5 m/pixel CTX orbital imagery from USGS Astrogeology, the app combines five geospatial layers: orbital imagery with multidirectional hillshade, elevation data, 200-metre contour lines, scientific reference sites, and a derived slope map.

To plan a path, the user selects a starting point and destination directly on the map, with the option to place up to 8 intermediate waypoints and reorder stops. The backend evaluates elevation change and terrain steepness to calculate route cost, producing smoothed multi-point trajectories across the crater.

Developed in a team of six. I worked on the Python backend, designing the pathfinding and slope-aware path cost algorithms.
