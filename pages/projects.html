Bordr

Live

Prepares photos for Instagram: a uniform border, the right format, and a carousel slicer for wide shots.

Bordr adds a uniform white border to your photos and exports them at your preferred ratio. Border width is set from the interface.

Wide photos can be sliced across 2 to 5 carousel slides, so a panorama becomes one continuous swipe. Results are exported together as a zip.

It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the Canvas API, with JSZip for packaging. Images are processed in your browser and never uploaded, and the page can be hosted statically with no login.

Stack
JavaScript · Canvas API · JSZip
Output
Variable (1:1, 4:5, 3:4, 16:9), zip
Slides
2 to 5 per wide photo
Hosting

Privacy
